# School Vault

School fees, kept safe and easy to follow.

School Vault is an app for schools in Uganda to record fee payments, give numbered receipts, and see who has paid and who still owes.

## Download

Tap this link to download the app for Android:

https://github.com/derrickebwosu-jpg/schoolvault-download/releases/latest/download/schoolvault.apk

## How to install

1. Tap the download link above and wait for the file schoolvault.apk to finish downloading.
2. Open the downloaded file.
3. If your phone asks, allow installing apps from this source, then tap Install.
4. Open School Vault, then sign in or create your school account.

## What it does

- Record fee payments and give numbered receipts
- Smart capture to add many payments quickly
- See which students owe and follow them up
- Reports: daily cash-up, fee statements, exam clearance, class collection and defaulters list
- History of every term and year, kept for ever
- Works on any Android phone
